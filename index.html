Evaluation build. Turn a folder of case files into one chronological PDF. Everything stays on your computer.

Download for Windows 64-bit installer · Windows 10 or 11 Download for Mac Apple Silicon (M1 and later) · macOS 11+

First time opening

These builds are not Apple-notarized yet. macOS will warn you. That is Gatekeeper, not malware in the file.

  • Windows: if SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.
  • Mac: drag to Applications, then open. If you see “Apple could not verify … is free of malware”:
    1. Click Done (or OK).
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    3. Scroll to the message about Desktop PDF Aggregator → Open Anyway → confirm.
    Or in Terminal:
    /usr/bin/xattr -cr "/Applications/Desktop PDF Aggregator.app"
    then open the app again.

Notes
